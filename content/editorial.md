## Ninety-Nine
I am co-editor of Global Justice Now's members magazine, [the Ninety-Nine] (https://www.globaljustice.org.uk/about-us/ninety-nine-our-members-magazine/) (‘challenging the power of the 1%’). I commission and work with writers from around the world engaged in incredible work: from [campaigners taking on a Google data centre in Uruguay] (https://www.globaljustice.org.uk/blog/2026/07/how-we-stood-up-to-a-google-data-centre/) to [movements in Greenland resisting Trump's threats]. (https://issuu.com/wdmuk/docs/ninety-nine_issue_34_-_march_2026) I also contribute pieces - such as [this feature] (https://www.globaljustice.org.uk/blog/2024/11/the-poorest-are-the-most-poisoned-inside-the-fight-for-justice-against-shell/) highlighting the Ogale and Bille communities' struggle for justice against Shell. 

## Progressive Review
While working at IPPR, I was on the editorial team for [Progressive Review] (https://www.ippr.org/progressive-review), IPPR's journal of politics and ideas. I commissioned and edited long-form pieces on a variety of areas: from [whether images can change our minds] (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/newe.12333), to [progress and setbacks on the path to LGBTQ+ equality] (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/newe.12325), to [critiques of Prevent] (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/newe.12311) to [diversity and media representation at a community level] (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/newe.12300).
